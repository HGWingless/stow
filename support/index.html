Support

  • Email is the fastest way to reach me: support@winglessenterprises.com. I reply within 2 business days.
  • Found a bug? Report it on GitHub Issues at github.com/HGWingless/stow/issues (needs a GitHub account). Never post your licence key there.
  • Before you write: open the sync's run history. A failed run says what went wrong and what to try.
  • Include your OS version and, if you can, the failed run's details.
  • Refunds and licence questions: email support@winglessenterprises.com. Refunds within 30 days, no questions asked.
